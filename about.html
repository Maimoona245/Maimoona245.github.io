About Zaina Boutique

A Lahore label built on craftsmanship, confidence and care.

Our Story

Zaina Boutique was founded in 2019 in a small rented studio in Gulberg, Lahore. Zainab Ahmed, a self-taught designer, began stitching outfits for friends and family because she couldn't find ready-to-wear that combined traditional embroidery with modern, wearable cuts.

Word spread quickly. What began as five orders a month became a full atelier with a team of tailors, embroiderers and designers, serving customers across Pakistan.

Our Journey

  • 2019
    Zainab opens a small studio in Gulberg and stitches her first 20 outfits.
  • 2021
    Launch of our first lawn collection and nationwide delivery.
  • 2023
    Bridal and formal line introduced, with hand embroidery by master artisans.
  • 2025
    Moved into our current boutique and expanded custom stitching to 10+ tailors.
