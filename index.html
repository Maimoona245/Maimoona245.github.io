Our Story

Zaina Boutique started in 2019 in Gulberg, Lahore, with one passion: making women feel confident and beautiful. Founder Zainab Ahmed began by designing outfits for friends and family, and her unique cuts quickly gained a loyal following.

Today we bring together traditional Pakistani craftsmanship and modern style. Every piece is made with premium fabrics, fine embroidery and careful stitching, so you look your best at every occasion.
